# Qualitative review

The qualitative analysis covers 46 cases. SE001–SE040 are a fixed purposive sample. SE041–SE046 are six Claude Sonnet 5 cases drawn from behavioral failures outside that sample.

Each case carries one primary theme:

- A: changed ambient assumptions
- B: incompatible supporting representations
- C: destination-specific obligations that require adaptation
- O: other case-specific relationships

`selected_cases.csv` lists the cases. `case_theme_mapping.csv` records the task, theme, and rationale.
